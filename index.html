Is Praisly allowed by Google?

Customers write and post reviews themselves. Praisly helps create optional draft text from customer input, and customers can edit or ignore the draft before posting.

Do customers need technical knowledge?

No. Customers scan a QR code, share their experience, and choose whether to continue to Google.
